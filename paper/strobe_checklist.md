**STROBE Statement: checklist of items that should be included in reports of cross-sectional studies**

**Manuscript**: Driver-conditioned transcriptomic differences across radiation-attributability bands in papillary thyroid carcinoma. Kotaro Harakawa, Vladimir A. Saenko, Norisato Mitsutake. Submitted to the *British Journal of Cancer*.

**How to read this checklist**: Page and line numbers refer to the manuscript file; line numbers are continuous and include the title page. Tables 1–3 and Figures 1–3 are separate files. Supplementary Methods, Supplementary Results, Figures S1–S2, Tables S1–S8 and Supplementary Data 1–2 are in the Supplementary Material.
